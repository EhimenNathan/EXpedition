# Third-party and competition materials

This private continuation repository includes small source-only references copied
from the KOZ-3 competition workspace so another authorized team member can
reproduce the experiments.

- `references/organizer_baseline/` and `references/organizer_simple/` come from
  organizer-provided starter packages. The competition's terms continue to
  govern those files.
- `experiments/codex/nvidia_pilot/cradio_v3b/` contains source used to reproduce
  a foundation-model experiment. Its upstream license and terms continue to
  apply.
- `legacy/previous_team/` preserves source from the earlier team workspace for
  auditability. It does not include datasets, trained weights, predictions, or
  credentials.

Do not make the repository public or redistribute competition data unless the
organizers' rules explicitly permit it.
